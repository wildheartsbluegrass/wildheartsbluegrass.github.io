A four-piece acoustic band based out of Toronto crafting renditions of Folk-rock, Americana, Celtic, and Old-Time music through the lens of Bluegrass. Drawing inspiration from artists such as David Grisman, The Grateful Dead, John Reischman, and Béla Fleck, Wildhearts fuses these sonic landscapes through meticulous orchestrations of traditional and contemporary pieces.

Instrumentation: upright bass, mandolin, guitar, banjo, violin, dobro, and occasionally trombone.
